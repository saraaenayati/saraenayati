in product.

I started in market research, then moved into customer experience. I spent time looking at competitors and listening to customer complaints before I started writing product requirements.

Today, I work on enterprise software at PMA. A lot of my work happens between a user asking for a change and a technical team needing a clear description of what to build.

PMA360 / Enterprise Digital Workplace

PMA360 brings internal system links, announcements, user guides and employee contacts into one portal. I work on its product direction and phased roadmap for seven subsidiaries.
